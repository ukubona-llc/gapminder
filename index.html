Gapminder's branch

Terminates at Representation. The chart is the decoder's output — legible, beautiful, and handed to a human to finish the job. No scalar, no verdict, no ledger. Trendalyzer got acquired by Google as a visualization feature, which is the correct outcome for a system that stops here.

Ukubona's branch

Continues through Eigenmode — the dominant driver of variance, protected — to Scalar. One number. Go / no-go. The prior updates. The loop closes and the event is written to the ledger, not left in someone's head.

P-03 · Constitutional Principle

The regulator is not optional. Every system needs a Matrix — a compression mechanism that filters signal from noise. Without it, you are flying the Tensor blind. Gapminder built the regulator and stopped one layer short of the verdict it made possible.
